# Objective

Build and, only under matching caller authority, publish a complete website
demo from this exact authored child graph. The result should feel
differentiated rather than cloned while preserving Ocean Flow, source-backed
mdkg claims, inspectable evidence, and fail-closed authority boundaries.

# End Condition

The goal is achieved when:

- the creative direction is recorded in `spike-1`;
- `task-1` has produced one portable static Astro `DemoOutput.astro` with zero
  client-side JavaScript or a clear blocked receipt;
- `test-1` records validation evidence for build, responsive rendering,
  accessibility, route completeness, zero client JavaScript, budgets,
  no-secret posture, and public-claims safety;
- valid caller authority, when present, gates `task-2` through `test-3`;
- integration, canonical validation, normal non-force publication, exact-SHA
  READY deployments, and public routes have compact receipts;
- a runtime checkpoint and goal evidence consistently name the completed chain,
  why it was performed, and what comes next.

# Non-Goals

- Do not infer integration, commit, push, deployment, or provider authority from
  this graph, its binding, local success, or a previous run.
- Do not force push, rewrite history, change DNS or provider configuration,
  manually redeploy, activate analytics, publish packages, or broaden paths.
- Do not store raw prompts, secrets, provider payloads, credentials, or private
  repo context.
- Do not treat the generated site as published or durable hosting.

# Recursive Algorithm

1. Verify the semantic release, `RUN_BINDING.json`, `CHILD_INTERFACE.json`, and
   `IMMUTABLE_CHILD_CONTRACT.json`.
2. Read `README.md`, `WEBSITE_DEMO_TEMPLATE_BRIEF.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `prd-2`,
   `dec-1`, `dec-2`, `edd-1`, and `chk-3`.
3. Use explicit `mdkg --root <run-root>` commands to select, claim, start,
   pack, execute, close, and evaluate one node at a time.
4. Complete `spike-1` with the audience, offer, structure, and creative
   direction without rewriting authored graph content.
5. Complete `task-1` with one portable output component or a clear blocker.
6. Complete `test-1` with validation evidence.
7. Require a matching caller-owned lease before `task-2` integration.
8. Build canonical output once and complete `test-2` checks serially.
9. Require a matching external human authority receipt before `task-3`.
10. Perform only the authorized bounded normal push.
11. Complete `test-3` exact-SHA and live-route verification read-only.
12. Seal the runtime checkpoint and evaluate the goal; otherwise record the
    precise blocker and stop truthfully.
