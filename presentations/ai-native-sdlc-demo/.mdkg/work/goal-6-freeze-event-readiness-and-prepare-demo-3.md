# Objective

Promote Demo 2's reusable lessons into a deterministic fork-ready source
release, then prepare a single-harness, 30-minute Demo 3 dress rehearsal
without hand-editing or executing its child graph.

# End Condition

Demo 2's measured execution and production evidence is converted into a
versioned timed-run contract and a classified reusable-delta decision. The
accepted source becomes a semantic release with the complete generic
positioning-to-production topology, root-explicit operator files, deterministic
receipt contracts, and a versioned immutable run-binding schema. Two distinct
fixtures prove zero-manual-edit absent-target creation and verify-only repeat.
The current deck is hash-frozen as the pre-test baseline with final polish
deferred. `runs/demo-003` is then created from the accepted source and frozen
binding, sealed, and left unexecuted. The exact single-harness sendoff,
prospective range policy, baseline-publication handoff, lease, human authority,
preflights, fallback, and dry rehearsal are sealed.

# Activation Conditions

Goal 5 has accepted checkpoint `chk-17`; `test-12`, `test-13`, and `test-14`
prove publication, production, and rehearsal/fallback outcomes. This
pre-activation enhancement and its checkpoint must be accepted. Any
source/operator/sendoff mutation recommended by `spike-6` still requires
explicit user acceptance before `task-47`.

# Non-Goals

- No Demo 3 implementation, commit, push, deployment, provider mutation, or
  canonical-site publication.
- No multi-harness execution, harness-equivalence proof, eight-hour/endurance
  demonstration, or claim that this rehearsal proves general long-horizon
  autonomy.
- No final post-test deck polish. Goal 6 freezes the current pre-test baseline;
  Goal 9 owns evidence-backed final polish after Demo 3.
- Goal 6 may apply only the exact source-template and sendoff refinements
  accepted after spike-6. It may not change mdkg CLI/package APIs, canonical
  site content, deployment configuration, docs, or unrelated source.
- No manual post-fork rewrite of Demo 3 goal, design, work, test, skill, or
  operator content. Reusable defects return to the source; run-specific inputs
  return to the binding; either change recreates an absent target.
- No Demo 2 positioning, navigation metaphor, marketing copy, runtime state,
  SHAs, deployment receipts, screenshots, or evidence may become source
  defaults.
- No child-authored authority. Human approval, Git leases, allowlists,
  published baselines, provider access, and validity windows remain external.

# Acceptance Criteria

- Demo 3 is an exact authored-content source fork with a frozen immutable run
  binding; no child work has started.
- The current deck, PPTX, notes, citations, renders, and rehearsal receipt are
  hash-frozen as a read-only pre-test baseline. Any material factual defect is
  recorded as a blocker requiring an explicit scope decision; deck
  regeneration, final polish, and approval occur only in Goal 9.
- Demo 2's actual execution and prompt behavior is evaluated before Demo 3.
  The accepted enhancement receipt records reusable changes, run-binding
  fields, child-local evidence, external authority, rejected Demo-2-specific
  choices, and explicit no-change rows where appropriate.
- The semantic source release inventories authored graph/operator/skill,
  README, and ignore inputs while excluding generated indexes, SQLite, events,
  packs, and runtime state from the authored identity.
- Two temporary absent-target fixtures with different IDs, routes, and
  positioning briefs prove the exact source, operator inventory, run-binding
  schema, fresh-agent packs, verify-only no-op, and cleanup. Neither fixture is
  retained.
- The source is `examples/website-demo-template/.mdkg/` at canonical `goal-1`;
  the target is `runs/demo-003/.mdkg/` with the same authored `goal-1` and
  complete chain. Demo 3 identity is supplied by the frozen run binding and
  interface receipt, not graph-body edits.
- Readiness evidence renders the reusable source contract beside the bound,
  unexecuted Demo 3 contract. Goal 7 later adds the positioning decision,
  statuses, work, and evidence that create the audience-facing contrast.
- The child goal exposes the complete positioning-to-production chain and contains no secrets or private refs.
- The immutable child-contract seal binds authored topology, role mapping,
  requirements, skills, sendoff and authority references, allowed receipt
  paths, and forbidden surfaces. Normal child statuses, events, evidence,
  indexes, packs, checkpoints, and outputs are explicitly mutable and do not
  invalidate the seal.
- The exact continue-until prompt, global deadline, repair limits, forbidden
  surfaces, and deterministic evidence outputs are sealed.
- Presentation/rehearsal kickoff defines `P0`. The orchestrator records `T0`
  immediately before invoking the exact child dispatch, no later than
  P0+00:45; the child acknowledges by T+00:45. Positioning is due by T+02,
  implementation by T+13, local critical validation by T+16, mechanical
  canonical integration by T+20, range proof and normal push by T+22, no new
  production repair after T+24, both exact-SHA deployments READY by T+26,
  routes and child checkpoint by T+28:30, consolidated reveal receipt by
  T+29:15, verified selection by T+29:30, and stop/select Demo 2 at T+30.
- At most two pre-publication repair cycles and one production fix-forward are
  allowed. The global deadline overrides remaining retries.
- Every timed stage records `started_at`, `completed_at`, `duration_ms`,
  `attempt_count`, retry class, `external_wait_ms`, interventions, blocker,
  and fallback selection.
- The accepted authority binds a prospective range-validation policy, not an
  invented future commit/range hash. A separately approved preparation
  baseline must be published and proven clean, with the integration lease
  released, before Goal 7 records `P0` or starts `T0`.
- A separate human-accepted `event-authority.json` pre-authorizes every frozen
  in-scope live action and confirms that no additional mid-run approval is
  required while its hashes and quiet window remain valid.
- The authority binds the source-release hash, run-binding hash, materializer
  or bootstrap version, immutable child seal, sendoff, allowlist, and range
  policy. The writable child contains only the read-only authority reference
  and cannot author or expand approval.
- The integration-owner quiet window has a start, end, invalidation condition, and owner.
- Dry rehearsal proves the workflow without side effects.
